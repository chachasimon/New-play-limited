JUMPY PRIVACY POLICY

Your Privacy Matters

1. Data Collection

Jumpy Game is built with a 'Privacy First' approach. We do not collect, store, or share any personal identifiable information (PII) from our users.

2. Local Storage Usage

The application uses local storage on your device (SharedPreferences) solely to save your personal high scores. This data never leaves your device.

3. Internet & Future Ads

While the current version of the game works entirely offline, future updates may include internet-based features such as advertisements or global leaderboards. If implemented, we will update this policy accordingly.

4. User Data Safety

Since no data is collected on our servers, there is no risk of your personal information being compromised through our application.

5. Contact & Support

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the game, you can contact us at support@jumpygame.com.
